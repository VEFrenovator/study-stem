# Privacy Policy

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to track general visitor traffic, page views, and navigation patterns to help us understand which notes are most useful.

## How Google Analytics Works
Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to collect anonymous usage data, including:
- Pages visited and time spent on each page.
- Device type, browser, and approximate geographic location (country/city level) derived from your IP address.

We do **not** collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name or email address. 

## Google's Data Collection and Processing
Google processes this data as a service provider. You can read more about how Google handles information and partner data by visiting [Google’s Privacy & Terms: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Opting Out
If you wish to prevent Google Analytics from collecting your visit data, you can install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=en) or configure your browser settings to block cookies.
