## The reason this site exists

Before I started running this channel, I have some problems with socialization. I truly want to tell loudly at least a bit of everything that is important to me. At first I was thinking about leading a telegram channel, as it do my friend. But the I understood that it does not suit me. Absence of LaTeX-formulas, impossibility of splitting content into theme groups, necessity of buying TG Premium — all these made me thinking of creating my own site, where I would can freely share my ideas in different fields of science and art.

### I want to discuss/comment an existing page

Unfortunately, the site itself does not support commenting, and all live-discussions were migrated on the [Project's GitHub](https://github.com/VEFrenovator/study-stem). So welcome in the [Discussions page](https://github.com/VEFrenovator/study-stem/discussions). There you will find a thread for each article; if one doesn’t exist yet, feel free to create your own.

### I have found a typo/bad English

It happens... English isn't my native language. On the [Issues page](https://github.com/VEFrenovator/study-stem/issues) create a new one (you can use `bad-english` template) or suggest correction using the [Pull requests page](https://github.com/VEFrenovator/study-stem/pulls).

### I have an idea for an article

Tell more about it in the [Discussions page](https://github.com/VEFrenovator/study-stem/discussions) or send me an [email](mailto:vladimir_e11@outlook.com).
